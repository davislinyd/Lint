What Lint doesLint 的特點

Lint lives in the menu bar and checks the English you write, wherever you write it.

Lint 住在選單列，在你寫字的任何地方檢查你的英文。

Works in any app在任何 App 裡都能用

Select text in Mail, Notes, a browser, Slack or any other app and press a shortcut. Browsers and Electron apps work too, and when a field exposes no text, Lint copies it through the clipboard and puts your clipboard back. The bubble opens on the Space you are in, fullscreen apps included.

在郵件、備忘錄、瀏覽器、Slack 或任何 App 選取文字，按下快捷鍵就行。瀏覽器與 Electron App 也支援；輸入框讀不到文字時，Lint 會透過剪貼簿複製，再把你的剪貼簿還原。浮窗會出現在你正在使用的 Space，包含全螢幕 App。

Two ways to review兩種檢查方式

⌥⌘K shows a small bubble with the suggestion; ⌥⌘L opens a side-by-side panel with every change marked. Replace your text in place, copy the result or ask for a rewrite. Lint starts preparing a suggestion as soon as you select text, so it is usually ready at once.

⌥⌘K 以小浮窗顯示建議；⌥⌘L 打開雙欄面板，標出每一處修改。可以直接取代原文、複製結果，或要求重寫。你一選取文字，Lint 就開始準備建議，所以通常馬上就好。

Careful English editing細心的英文潤飾

Proofread & polish fixes grammar, wrong words and spelling, and tries to keep correct wording, names, numbers and your layout as they are. Pick a tone (Preserve Tone, Formal, Concise or Professional), use Translate to read English text in the translation language you choose (Traditional Chinese by default), or write a Custom prompt.

「文法校對與潤飾」修正文法、用錯的字與拼字，並盡量保留正確的用字、人名、數字與版面。可以選語氣（保留原語氣、正式、簡潔或專業），用「翻譯」把英文翻成你選的翻譯語言來閱讀（預設繁體中文），或寫「自訂 Prompt」。

Your text stays on your Mac文字留在你的 Mac 上

Lint's local AI (a bundled llama.cpp running Gemma 4 E4B) and Apple Intelligence both run on the device; the network is only used to download the model and to check GitHub for Lint updates. If you prefer, point Lint at an OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as Ollama or LM Studio; an API key, if it needs one, is kept in the Keychain.

Lint 的本機 AI（內建 llama.cpp 執行 Gemma 4 E4B）和 Apple Intelligence 都在這台 Mac 上執行，只有下載模型和到 GitHub 檢查 Lint 更新時需要網路。也可以改接 Ollama、LM Studio 等 OpenAI 相容端點；需要 API Key 的話，Key 存在鑰匙圈。

Memory and dreaming (optional)記憶與作夢（選用）

Turn on Memory and Lint writes down the mistakes you correct as short rules, stored only on your Mac, reminds the model of them from the next suggestion on, and forgets the ones that don’t come back while it dreams. You can read and delete every memory. How memory and dreaming work

開啟記憶後，Lint 會把你修正過的錯誤記成簡短規則，只存在你的 Mac，下一次建議就開始提醒模型，沒有再出現的會在作夢時忘記。每一條記憶都能查看與刪除。記憶與作夢怎麼運作

Made to fit you依你的習慣調整

Change both shortcuts, switch the interface to one of nine languages, and edit the instructions for each mode and tone.

兩組快捷鍵都能更改，介面可以切換成九種語言之一，每個模式與語氣的指示也都能修改。

Before you start開始之前

  • A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • For Lint's own local AI: about 6.2 GB of free disk space (the default model is about 5.15 GB), and an internet connection for that one download.
  • Or, on macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on, Lint can use Apple Intelligence instead, with nothing to download.
  • 搭載 Apple 晶片、執行 macOS 14 Sonoma 或更新版本的 Mac。
  • 使用 Lint 自己的本機 AI：約 6.2 GB 可用空間（預設模型約 5.15 GB），以及下載這一次所需的網路。
  • 或者，在 macOS 26 以上且已開啟 Apple Intelligence 的 Mac，Lint 可以改用 Apple Intelligence，不必下載。

Memory and dreaming記憶與作夢

Optional, and off by default. With it on, Lint notices writing habits you keep correcting, such as a word you often misspell or an article you often drop, and writes each one down as a short rule. While it is off, Lint behaves exactly as it would without it. Dreaming is what later keeps a memory, forgets it, or sums several of them up.

選用功能，預設關閉。開啟後，Lint 會留意你反覆修正的寫作習慣，例如常拼錯的字、常漏掉的冠詞，把每一個記成簡短的規則。關閉時，Lint 的行為與沒有這個功能時完全相同。作夢是之後把記憶留下、忘記，或把好幾條收成一條的時候。

Open Settings… → Memory and turn on Enable Memory. The counts start at 0 and grow as you use Lint.

How a memory is formed記憶怎麼形成

  • A memory comes from what you do with a suggestion. Editing the result before you replace your text counts the most, because those are your own words; replacing counts less, copying a little, and asking for a rewrite records nothing.
  • A new memory is used from the next suggestion on: it is short-term. If its pattern comes back within 7 days, or the model applies it and you accept that, it becomes long-term; if not, it is forgotten while dreaming. A change the model made that depends on its sentence, such as “tickets” for “ticket”, is used only once it comes back. Undoing what a memory asks for counts twice as much against it, and forgets it at once unless it is well established.
  • When you write, Lint adds the few memories that fit your text, at most five short lines, to the end of the prompt. With Lint's local AI or Apple Intelligence they never leave your Mac; with a cloud provider they are sent along with your text.
  • 記憶來自你怎麼處理建議。在取代前先修改結果最有份量，因為那是你自己的用字；直接取代次之，複製只算一點，要求重寫則不會記下任何東西。
  • 新記下的記憶從下一次建議就開始使用，這是短期記憶。7 天內同樣的樣式又出現，或模型照提醒改了而你接受，它就轉成長期記憶；沒有的話就在作夢時忘記。模型改的、要看句子而定的修正（例如「tickets」改成「ticket」）要再出現一次才會使用。你若推翻某條記憶要求的改法，扣分是加分的兩倍，而且除非它已經很穩固，否則會立刻忘記。
  • 你寫作時，Lint 只把符合這段文字的少數記憶（最多五行短句）加在 prompt 結尾。使用 Lint 本機 AI 或 Apple Intelligence 時，它們不會離開你的 Mac；使用雲端服務時，會隨你的文字一起送出。

What is kept會記下什麼

  • Everything stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Lint/LintLearning.sqlite, readable only by you. The settings page calls this Memory; the file name stays LintLearning.sqlite.
  • Only abstracted rules are kept: a word, a short phrase or a template sentence, never the text around it. Numbers, addresses, paths, acronyms and capitalized names are left out.
  • Lint cannot recognize Chinese names or names typed in lowercase, so a name you corrected may still end up in a memory. You can find and delete it under Manage Memories….
  • What you did with a suggestion is logged as keyed hashes, not text; the key is in your Keychain.
  • 全部只存在你的 Mac：~/Library/Application Support/Lint/LintLearning.sqlite，僅你本人可讀。設定頁現在叫記憶，檔名仍是 LintLearning.sqlite。
  • 只保存抽象後的規則：一個字、一小段詞組或模板句，不保存周圍的文字。數字、網址、路徑、縮寫與大寫開頭的人名不會被記下。
  • Lint 認不出中文人名或全小寫的英文名，被你修正過的名字仍可能進入記憶；可以在「管理記憶…」找到並刪除。
  • 你對建議做了什麼，只以帶金鑰的雜湊記錄，不含文字；金鑰放在你的鑰匙圈。

Dreaming作夢

Dreaming writes down which memories proved themselves and which are forgotten, and erases traces that have faded. A forgotten memory is no longer used. Only a trace is kept, so that Lint recognizes the pattern, and keeps it at once, if it comes back. A long-term memory that stops showing up fades: after a month without it, its evidence halves every three months (every six months for a general rule, every year for a core memory), and below half of where it started it is forgotten. A trace that has faded away is erased for good, roughly three to eleven months after the pattern was last seen. Pinned and disabled memories don't fade, neither they nor memories you reworded are ever erased, and Enable brings a forgotten memory back.

When several memories of one kind pile up, a dream sums them up in one general rule. For now it handles one kind: a preposition you keep deleting after different verbs, such as “discuss about” and “mention about”. The rule is worded from a fixed template, so it never contains a word you wrote. The memories behind it are kept and come back if the rule fades, is disabled or is deleted; pinned, disabled and hand-edited memories are never combined, and a rule you delete is not made again. A rule that keeps proving itself for weeks becomes a core memory.

Dreaming runs only on your Mac, when you are not waiting for a suggestion: at least once a day while you use Lint (a minute after it starts, or after your first feedback of the day), after enough new memories, or when you click Dream Now, which then shows how many memories it remembered, forgot and erased.

作夢會記下哪些記憶證明有用、哪些要忘記，並刪除已經淡掉的痕跡。忘記的記憶不再使用，只留下痕跡，讓 Lint 在同樣的樣式又出現時認出來並直接留下。長期記憶不再出現就會淡出：超過一個月沒出現後，證據每三個月減半（一般規則每六個月、核心記憶每一年），低於起始的一半就忘記。淡掉的痕跡會被永久刪除，大約是最後一次出現後三到十一個月。已釘選與已停用的記憶不會淡出，它們和你改寫過的記憶都不會被刪除；按「啟用」可以把忘記的記憶找回來。

同一類的記憶累積多條時，作夢會把它們歸納成一條一般規則。目前只處理一類：你在不同動詞後反覆刪掉的介系詞，例如「discuss about」與「mention about」。規則由固定模板寫成，不會含有任何你寫過的字。被歸納的記憶都會保留，規則淡出、停用或被刪除後就會恢復；已釘選、已停用與你手動改寫過的記憶不會被歸納，你刪掉的規則也不會再被產生。持續數週都證明有用的規則會升為核心記憶。

作夢只在你的 Mac 上進行，並等到你不在等建議時才開始：使用 Lint 期間至少每天一次（啟動一分鐘後，或當天第一次回饋後）、累積足夠的新記憶之後，或按下「現在作夢」，完成後會顯示記住、忘記與刪除了幾條。

Manage your memories管理記憶

Manage Memories… lists every memory. You can edit, pin, disable, enable or delete each one, and see whether it is short-term, long-term or forgotten (forgotten ones are listed when you turn on Show forgotten memories), whether it is specific, general or core, and which memories a rule came from. Clear All Memories… deletes every memory but keeps the feedback log; Reset Memory Database… deletes the memories and the log, and cannot be undone.

「管理記憶…」會列出每一條記憶。每一條都可以編輯、釘選、停用、啟用或刪除，也能看到它是短期、長期還是已忘記（打開「顯示已忘記的記憶」才會列出已忘記的）、是具體、一般還是核心記憶，以及一般規則由哪些記憶歸納而來。「清除所有記憶…」會刪除所有記憶但保留回饋紀錄；「重設記憶資料庫…」會刪除記憶與回饋紀錄，無法復原。

Troubleshooting疑難排解

Lint cannot read or replace textLint 讀不到或取代不了文字

Check Settings → General → Accessibility. After an update macOS may treat Lint as a new app: in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, remove the old Lint entries, click + and add /Applications/Lint.app again.

檢查「設定 → 一般 → 輔助功能」。更新後 macOS 可能把 Lint 當成新的 App：到「系統設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 輔助功能」刪除舊的 Lint 項目，按 ＋ 重新加入 /Applications/Lint.app。

Replace says “Nothing was written.”取代時顯示「沒有寫入。」

Lint only writes over the exact text it checked. If that text has changed, moved or is no longer selected, Lint leaves the field as it is. Select the text again and check it once more.

Lint 只會寫在它檢查過的那段原文上。原文如果改過、移動了，或已經不在選取範圍內，Lint 就不動輸入框。重新選取文字，再檢查一次。

Replace says it could not confirm the text was written取代時顯示「無法確認已寫入」

The field could be read before the replace but did not show the new text afterwards. Look at the field, and undo with ⌘Z if it is wrong.

取代前讀得到輸入框，取代後卻看不到新文字。請看一下輸入框，不對的話按 ⌘Z 還原。

macOS asks to let Lint control “System Events”macOS 詢問是否允許 Lint 控制「系統事件」

Allow it. Lint only uses it to bring your previous app back to the front before it replaces text, which makes Replace reliable in browsers and Electron apps.

請允許。Lint 只用它在取代文字前，把你原本使用的 App 帶回最前面，這樣在瀏覽器與 Electron App 裡取代才穩定。

The first suggestion takes a while第一個建議要等一下

The model is loading. On a Mac that is short of memory this can take minutes; Lint waits up to 10 minutes. After 5 minutes without a suggestion the model leaves memory, and the next request loads it again in a few seconds.

模型正在載入。在記憶體吃緊的 Mac 上可能要幾分鐘，Lint 最多等 10 分鐘。5 分鐘沒有要求建議時，模型會先釋放記憶體，下次要求時幾秒內重新載入。

The download stopped下載中斷了

Choose Set Up Local AI… in the menu bar menu and download again: it continues where it stopped. A file that fails its check is deleted and never used.

在選單列選單點「設定本機 AI…」再下載一次，會從中斷處繼續。驗證失敗的檔案會被刪除，絕不會使用。

The pencil icon is not in the menu bar選單列上找不到鉛筆圖示

When the menu bar is full, macOS hides some icons. Close a few menu bar apps, check System Settings → Menu Bar on newer macOS, or look in your menu bar manager (such as Bartender or Ice). Lint's shortcuts work either way.

選單列太滿時，macOS 會隱藏部分圖示。可以關掉幾個選單列 App；在較新的 macOS 上檢查「系統設定 → 選單列」；或到你的選單列管理工具（例如 Bartender、Ice）裡找。不管圖示有沒有顯示，Lint 的快捷鍵都能用。

Not enough disk space磁碟空間不足

Before downloading, Lint checks for about 1.2 times what is still missing and tells you how much it needs and how much your Mac has.

下載前，Lint 會檢查是否有「還缺的部分 ×1.2」的空間，並告訴你需要多少、這台 Mac 有多少。

A new version is found but not installed找到新版卻沒有安裝

With Ask before installing (the default), choose Download and Install in Settings → General → Updates or in the menu bar menu. Lint only replaces a copy in /Applications or ~/Applications that is signed with Lint's Developer ID; otherwise it offers Open Release Page, where you can download the DMG yourself.

在「手動更新」（預設）下，到「設定 → 一般 → 更新」或選單列選單按「下載並安裝」。Lint 只會取代放在 /Applications 或 ~/Applications、並以 Lint 的 Developer ID 簽署的那一份；其他情況會提供「打開釋出頁面」，讓你自己下載 DMG。
