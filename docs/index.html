Lint

A macOS menu-bar AI writing assistant. macOS 選單列 AI 寫作助手。

Press a global hotkey to capture the selected text, send it to an LLM, compare the suggestion in a floating panel, then replace or copy it. 全域快捷鍵擷取選取文字，送到 LLM，在浮動面板對比後覆蓋或複製。

Features功能

Works everywhere隨處可用

Native apps are read through Accessibility. Chromium and Electron apps — browsers, Slack, SeaTalk, ChatGPT — are switched on automatically.

原生 App 透過輔助功能讀取；瀏覽器、Slack、SeaTalk、ChatGPT 等 Chromium／Electron App，Lint 會自動開啟其輔助功能樹。

Live background suggestions背景即時建議

While you type, Lint watches the focused field and prepares a suggestion in the background, so the hotkey shows it right away.

打字時 Lint 監看目前輸入框，在背景先準備好建議，按下快捷鍵即可立刻顯示。

Writing modes寫作模式

Proofreading and polishing, formal / concise / professional tone, translation, and a custom prompt.

文法校對與潤飾、正式／簡潔／專業語氣、翻譯，以及自訂 Prompt。

Local AI built in本機 AI 內建

llama.cpp ships inside Lint — no Homebrew — and Lint downloads a model only after you agree. Or use OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — Ollama, LM Studio, custom.

llama.cpp 已包在 Lint 裡（不需要 Homebrew），同意後才下載模型。也可改用 OpenAI、Anthropic、Gemini，或任何 OpenAI 相容端點——Ollama、LM Studio、自訂端點。

Rebindable hotkeys快捷鍵可重新綁定

⌥⌘K and ⌥⌘L are the defaults — rebind either one in Settings.

⌥⌘K 與 ⌥⌘L 是預設值，可在設定中重新綁定。

Keychain-securedKeychain 加密存放

API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain, never in plain text.

API Key 存放在 macOS 鑰匙圈，絕不以明文儲存。

Optional local learning可選的本機學習

Lint can learn writing habits you keep correcting and remember them as short, abstracted rules — and fold similar ones into fewer general rules. Off by default, fully inspectable and deletable.

可讓 Lint 記住你反覆修正的寫作習慣，變成簡短規則，還能把相近的規則歸納得更精簡——預設關閉，隨時可查看與刪除。

Fullscreen aware全螢幕感知

The suggestion bubble opens on whichever Space you're working in, including fullscreen apps.

浮動視窗會出現在你正在使用的 Space，包含全螢幕 App。

Learning, kept private學習機制，隱私優先

Turn on Settings → Learning and Lint notices writing habits you keep correcting — a word you often misspell, an article you often drop — and remembers them as short rules. It's off by default, and behaves exactly the same either way. 在「設定 → 學習」開啟後，Lint 會留意你反覆修正的寫作習慣（常拼錯的字、常漏掉的冠詞），記成簡短規則；預設關閉，關閉時行為完全不變。

  • Everything stays on your Mac, in a local database only you can read.全部只存在你的 Mac，僅你本人可讀。
  • Only abstracted rules are kept — a word or short phrase — never the surrounding text.只保存抽象化後的規則（一個字或一小段詞組），不保存周圍的文字。
  • Memories fade automatically once a pattern stops showing up.不再出現的規則會自動淡出。
  • Similar memories can be folded into one general rule, on your Mac only — the originals are kept and come back if the rule is disabled or deleted.相近的記憶可歸納成一條一般規則，只在你的 Mac 上進行；原本的記憶都保留，規則停用或刪除後就會恢復。
  • Every memory can be viewed, pinned, disabled, or deleted — or the whole database reset.每一條記憶都能查看、釘選、停用或刪除，也能整個重設。
